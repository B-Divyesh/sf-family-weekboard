# Weekboard copy audit

Audited 2026-08-28 after polish round 1. Counts treat hyphenated words as one.
Every reviewed sentence is 22 words or fewer. The banned marketing terms do not
appear in visitor copy.

## First screen

| Copy | Words |
| --- | ---: |
| Plan your family week together | 5 |
| For families using phones, computers, and paper who need one shared weekly view without a new account. | 17 |
| Works offline after the first visit. | 6 |
| Your schedule stays on this device. | 6 |
| Adding plans, printing, and both exports are free. | 8 |
| Try it with sample data | 5 |
| Opens a separate sample board. | 5 |

The first screen states the job, audience, next action, offline boundary,
privacy boundary, and free core functions without asking visitors to interpret
the visual theme.
