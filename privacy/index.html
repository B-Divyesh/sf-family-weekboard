PLAIN-LANGUAGE POLICY · 28 AUGUST 2026

Privacy

Your household schedule belongs to your household. Weekboard is designed so we do not receive it.

Data stored on your device

People, plans, notes, board preferences, and an optional license token stay in this browser. They remain until you delete site data, remove them, or replace the board during an import.

Exports and handoffs

ICS and Weekboard exports are created locally. Encrypted copies use AES-GCM encryption in your browser. Files, codes, and QR images go only where you choose to send them. Anyone with both an encrypted copy and its passphrase can read it.

Network requests

The free planner requires no account and makes no API request. It has no advertising, analytics, tracking pixel, or third-party font. If you enter a supporter license, the token goes to the Sociobot billing API only to check whether it is valid. A purchase opens Sociobot’s hosted checkout.

Your controls

Export an ICS copy at any time. To erase all local data, clear storage for this site in your browser. Uninstalling the PWA may not clear browser storage on every platform.

Contact

For product or privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
