Weekboard is a local planning utility, not a hosted calendar or emergency notification service.

Using Weekboard

You may use the application for lawful personal or household planning. You are responsible for maintaining your own exports and for checking that imported plans, time zones, and recurring events match the source calendar.

No live sync

ICS files, encrypted files, and QR handoffs are snapshots. They do not update another device automatically. Importing an encrypted Weekboard copy replaces the current board on that device after confirmation.

Supporter purchase

The supporter pack is a one-time ₹499 purchase that adds a custom board name, extra lane colours, and more than four people. Core scheduling, accessibility, printing, encryption, and export remain free. Sociobot / Dodo is the merchant of record and handles payment and refunds. A refunded, expired, or revoked license may stop unlocking supporter extras without affecting existing plans or core exports.

The software is provided “as is” without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability. Browser storage can be cleared by device settings, storage pressure, or private-browsing policies. Keep exports for plans you cannot afford to lose.
