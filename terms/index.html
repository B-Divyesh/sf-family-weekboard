Weekboard is a local planning utility, not a hosted calendar or emergency notification service.

Using Weekboard

You may use the application for lawful personal or household planning. You are responsible for maintaining your own exports and for checking that imported plans, time zones, and recurring events match the source calendar.

No live sync

ICS files, encrypted files, and QR copies are snapshots. They do not update another device automatically. Importing an encrypted Weekboard copy replaces the current board on that device after confirmation.
